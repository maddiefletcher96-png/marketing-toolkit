# Rebrand comms rollout plan

A framework for telling customers, partners and staff about a rebrand without confusing anyone. Use it for a name change, a new identity or a merger of brands.

The idea is simple: **tell people in the right order, in phases, and tailor each message to what that audience actually needs to know.**

## The three phases

| Phase | When | Goal | What goes out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Before** | 2-4 weeks before launch | No surprises | Staff briefing and FAQ, partner and supplier letters, a heads-up for existing bookers or customers |
| **2. Launch** | Launch day and week | One clear story | Launch email to the full database, social channel switchover, website live, press release |
| **3. After** | Weeks 2-8 | Make it stick | Follow-ups by audience, "what's changed and what hasn't" content, a one-month-in report |

## Segment by what people need to know

Write one version of each message per audience, not one email for everyone. A customer with a booking next week has different questions from someone who stayed once three years ago.

| Audience | Their main question | What to tell them |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | "What do I say to customers?" | The story in two lines, an FAQ, the date everything changes |
| Upcoming bookers | "Is my booking still valid?" | Yes, and exactly what will look different when they arrive |
| Past customers | "Why should I care?" | What's better, and a reason to come back |
| Members or loyalty | "Do I keep my benefits?" | What carries over, and anything new |
| Partners and suppliers | "What do I need to change?" | Logos, invoices, contact details, deadlines |
| Press | "What's the story?" | Why it's happening and why now |

Map phases against audiences and you get your template list. Plan it as a grid before you write anything.

## Social switchover

- Switch every channel on the same day, in a planned order
- Update handles, names, bios, profile images, links and pinned posts in one go
- Keep old handles parked so nobody else takes them
- Post a short "we've changed our name" explainer, pinned for the first few weeks
- Prepare answers for the comments you know you'll get

## The one-month-in report

A short deck for leadership, not a data dump:

1. What we set out to do
2. What went out, and when
3. What happened: email performance, social follower and engagement change, site traffic, press coverage, customer feedback
4. What surprised us
5. What's next

## Checklist

- [ ] Staff briefed before anyone outside the business
- [ ] Every audience mapped to a message for each phase
- [ ] Upcoming bookers told exactly what changes for them
- [ ] All channels switched on the same day, old handles parked
- [ ] Website redirects tested
- [ ] FAQ ready for front-line teams
- [ ] One-month-in report scheduled
