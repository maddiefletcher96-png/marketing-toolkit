# PR familiarisation stay tracker

A tracker for press and journalist stays at hotels, restaurants and venues. Open [`tracker-template.csv`](tracker-template.csv) in Google Sheets or Excel, one row per guest.

## Why not just use an influencer sheet?

Most agencies hand over an influencer tracker, which is full of columns that don't apply to a journalist: social handles, follower counts, engagement rate, content deadlines, hashtags, EMV. I strip those out and keep what the venue team actually needs to look after the guest:

- **Who:** name, publication, contact details
- **Logistics:** property, dates, nights, number of guests, room type
- **What's included:** what's complimentary (breakfast, dinner, credit), and who's paying for what
- **Care:** dietary and accessibility needs
- **Outcome:** expected coverage, publish date, link once it's live

## Rules I follow

- **Pull the details from the email thread,** not from memory.
- **Mark anything unknown as "To confirm"** (room type and dietary needs are the usual ones). Never fill a gap with a guess, since the venue will act on it.
- **One row per guest.** If the venue team needs separate briefs, make one sheet per guest.
- **Share the sheet with the venue** a few days before arrival, so reception isn't surprised.
