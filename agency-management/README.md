# Agency management kit

Templates for managing social media and PR agencies from the client side: a monthly scorecard, a content sign-off flow and a monthly report outline. They're meant to keep the relationship good, and to give you a clear record if it isn't.

## Monthly scorecard

Score each line 1-5 every month, with a note. Use [`scorecard-template.csv`](scorecard-template.csv).

| Area | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **Delivery** | Content and reports arrive on the agreed dates |
| **Quality** | On brand first time, few rounds of changes |
| **Results** | Hits the KPIs agreed in the contract |
| **Ideas** | Brings ideas you didn't ask for |
| **Communication** | Responsive, flags problems early, no surprises |
| **Value** | Hours and outputs match the fee |

A scorecard written up every month is what makes a contract review fair, for both sides.

## Content sign-off flow

1. **Brief.** Client sends a brief with the goal, key messages, dates and must-haves.
2. **Plan.** Agency sends a monthly content plan at least two weeks before the month starts.
3. **Feedback.** Client gives one round of consolidated feedback within an agreed number of working days.
4. **Final.** Agency sends final content. Client approves or rejects, and doesn't reopen general feedback.
5. **Publish.** Agency schedules and confirms what's gone live.
6. **Report.** Monthly report (outline below).

Rules that help:

- One person on the client side gives the feedback, so the agency doesn't get conflicting notes
- Anything urgent outside the plan is logged as extra work
- Missed deadlines are written down at the time, not remembered at review

## Monthly report outline

Ask the agency for this in the same order every month:

1. Headline numbers against target (reach, engagement, followers, clicks, coverage)
2. Top three and bottom three pieces of content, and why
3. What was delivered against the plan
4. Anything that slipped, and why
5. Next month's focus

## Contract review checklist

- [ ] Scorecards for each month of the contract
- [ ] KPIs from the contract compared with actual results
- [ ] Log of missed deadlines and extra work
- [ ] Fee compared with hours and outputs delivered
- [ ] Decision: renew, renegotiate the scope, or retender
