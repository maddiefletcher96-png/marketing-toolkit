## How to use this skill

1. **Read the principles before drafting.** The three principles below are the substance of the voice.
2. **Calibrate to the user's examples first.** If the user shares their own writing as a reference, it outranks everything in this file. Match its rhythm, paragraph length, pronouns and sentence fragments.
3. **Draft.**
4. **Run the checklist.** Every draft goes through `checklist.md` before it's delivered. If a check fails, revise and check again. This step isn't optional.

## Common failure modes

- **AI-tell phrases.** "It's not just X, it's Y", "a testament to", "immerse yourself", "elevate your experience". Cut on sight. Full list in `checklist.md`.
- **Bookending with the brand.** Opening or closing on "we" or the brand name. Bring the reader in first.
- **Leading with the credential.** An award or certificate means more after the work it stands for.
