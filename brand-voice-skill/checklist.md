# Final review checklist

Every draft goes through this before it's delivered. If a check fails, revise and run it again. Deliver the clean final, not a tracked-changes version. If you've bent a rule on purpose, say so briefly so the person can decide.

## The four questions

### 1. Does it sound like a person?

Read it out loud. It should sound like someone talking to a reader they respect, not a brochure.

Fail signals: marketing register, formal corporate rhythm, anything written for a brand rather than a reader.

### 2. Is the reader's benefit the focus?

Say what the reader gets or what actually happens, not how the brand feels about itself.

Fail signals: "we're proud to", "we strive to", "our aim is", "we showcase".

### 3. Is it specific?

Swap anything vague for a detail: a time, a place, a number, a name. Claims without specifics read as marketing.

### 4. Have I cut the filler?

- Superlatives ending in -est (best, finest, greatest)
- Filler words (very, really, basically, quite, simply)
- Self-flattering adjectives (carefully crafted, thoughtfully designed, beautifully appointed)
- Generic descriptors (vibrant, bustling, stunning, enchanting)

If you delete the adjective and the sentence still holds, leave it deleted.

## The AI-phrase scan

Cut on sight:

- "This is more than a [thing], it's a..."
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "This is an invitation to..."
- "A testament to..."
- Elevate, unleash, unlock, boost
- "Rich tapestry of..."
- "Immerse yourself..."
- "A truly unforgettable experience"
- "[Category] redefined"
- "Your own private oasis"
- Seamlessly blend, harmoniously integrate
- "Discover a new level of..."
- "The art of...", "The joy of..."
- Unparalleled, unrivalled, second to none
- Doubled-up synonyms ("the serene, tranquil space") - use one strong word
- Em dashes used for drama in every other sentence

## Structural checks

- Pronouns, questions, headlines, dates, times and contractions follow the quick rules in `SKILL.md`
- No quotes from famous people unless your rules allow them
- The brand name isn't the first or last word of the piece

## Claims check

- Every fact, number, award or detail comes from a verified source. Nothing invented.
- Nothing from the source has been dropped unless someone asked for it to go.

## Brand rules

Finally, check the draft against the quick rules and banned words in `SKILL.md`.
