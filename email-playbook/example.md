# Worked example

A fictional city hotel group, Northbank Hotels, with two sites. The offer and events are made up for illustration.

---

**Subject:** The weekends that go first this winter

Hi [First name],

Every year it's the same. The Christmas market weekends are fully booked by October, the New Year rooms go in a week, and anyone who leaves it late ends up staying an hour out of town.

So here's a reason to book now. Book two nights with us and the third is free.

Why a third night? Because winter trips are better slow. When it's dark at four, a rushed two-day visit means seeing the market in the cold and leaving before the lights come on. An extra night gives you the evening.

**What's on**

**Northbank Riverside**
- Christmas market, 20th November to 23rd December
- Lights trail along the river, every evening from 1st December

Both are a ten-minute walk away, so you can head out after dinner and be back before your hands get cold.

**Northbank Old Town**
- Carol concert at the cathedral, 18th December
- New Year's Eve fireworks over the castle

The cathedral is around the corner, and the fireworks are visible from the top-floor rooms. Ask for one when you book.

The market weekends and New Year's Eve always go first. The offer ends 28th February 2027.

[Book your stay]

See you soon,
[Name]
Northbank Hotels
