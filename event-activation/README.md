# Local event activation checklist

How to use a local festival, sports event or city-wide moment to drive bookings and content for a hotel, restaurant or venue. It isn't about sponsorship, just being ready when the city is busy.

## 6-8 weeks before

- [ ] Confirm the dates, the footfall expected and where things happen
- [ ] Decide the goal: bookings, food and drink covers, content, local awareness, or a mix
- [ ] Agree the offer, if there is one (e.g. a food and drink credit for creators, a set menu, late checkout)
- [ ] Brief your social or PR agency, with deadlines that leave room for slippage
- [ ] Build a shortlist of local creators who already post about the event or the area
- [ ] Check what's allowed: event branding, music licensing, filming in public spaces

## 3-4 weeks before

- [ ] Send creator outreach: short, personal, clear on the offer and what you'd love in return
- [ ] Plan the content: what you need captured, by whom, and on which days
- [ ] Brief the on-site team on what's happening and how they can help with content
- [ ] Line up any partners (a local brewery, a band, a market stall)
- [ ] Schedule pre-event posts and an email to locals and past guests

## The week of

- [ ] Kit ready for on-site capture: phone, gimbal, charged batteries, a simple shot list
- [ ] A named person on-site each day for content
- [ ] Creator visits confirmed, with arrival details sent
- [ ] A daily check-in with the agency on what's been posted

## During

- Capture more than you think you need: people, food, the street, behind the scenes
- Post in the moment on stories, and save the polished pieces for afterwards
- Reshare creator content quickly (with permission)

## After

- [ ] Thank every creator and partner within a few days
- [ ] Gather all content into one shared folder
- [ ] Post a recap
- [ ] Report: bookings or covers against the same period last year, reach, creator posts, what worked and what didn't

## If the agency delivers late

It happens. Plan for it:

- Set agency deadlines a week before you actually need things
- Have a basic in-house fallback: your own team captures on-site, you post simply
- Write down what slipped and why, and bring it to the next agency review (see [`agency-management`](../agency-management))

## Creator outreach template

> Hi [Name],
>
> I love your posts about [area or event], especially [a specific post]. We're [venue] on [street], and we'd love to host you during [event].
>
> We'd like to offer [offer, e.g. a food and drink credit for two]. In return, we'd love [ask, e.g. a story or two while you're with us], but no pressure on format.
>
> Would [dates] work for you?
>
> [Your name]
