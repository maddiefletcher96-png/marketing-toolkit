# Marketing toolkit

Templates, playbooks and AI workflows for marketing and comms in hospitality and consumer brands. Everything here has been stripped back to work for any brand, so take what's useful.

| Folder | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [`brand-voice-skill`](brand-voice-skill) | Turn a brand book into a reusable AI writing skill, with a review checklist that catches generic, AI-sounding copy | Brand and content teams using Claude or other AI tools |
| [`menu-builder-skill`](menu-builder-skill) | A step-by-step AI workflow for building a restaurant or café menu in Canva from a house template, proofreading it and putting it online | Hospitality marketers, small food businesses |
| [`email-playbook`](email-playbook) | How I write offer-led marketing emails: start with the reader's problem, state the terms plainly, one call to action | Anyone writing CRM or newsletter emails |
| [`pr-stay-tracker`](pr-stay-tracker) | A tracker for press and journalist familiarisation stays, adapted from an influencer sheet | Hotel, travel and hospitality PR |
| [`property-descriptions`](property-descriptions) | Rules (and a skill) for hotel and booking-site descriptions: verified sources only, nothing dropped, neighbourhood first | Hotel, apartment and venue marketers |
| [`rebrand-rollout`](rebrand-rollout) | A phased plan for announcing a rebrand to staff, customers, partners and press, with an email plan template | Anyone running a rebrand or name change |
| [`event-activation`](event-activation) | A week-by-week checklist for using a local festival or event to drive bookings and content | Hospitality and local consumer brands |
| [`agency-management`](agency-management) | A monthly scorecard, content sign-off flow and report outline for managing social and PR agencies | Client-side marketers who manage agencies |

## Why this exists

Most of a marketer's craft lives in decks, inboxes and agency calls, where nobody else can see it. These are the bits I kept coming back to, written up so they can be reused.

The skills (the `SKILL.md` files) are written for [Claude](https://claude.ai), but the thinking in them works as a plain brief for any AI tool or any new team member.
